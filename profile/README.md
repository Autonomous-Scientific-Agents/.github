### What we work on

- **Agentic computational chemistry**: LLM agents that plan and run quantum chemistry and materials simulations, plus benchmarks to measure how well they do it.
- **Reproducible science**: agents that read papers, rebuild their computational environments, and regenerate their code.
- **Tools and data for chemistry and ML**: workflow frameworks, molecular identifiers, and dashboards for large simulation datasets.
- **Training and outreach**: guides and directories that help students and researchers use AI tools in their work.
